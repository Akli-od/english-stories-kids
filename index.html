    💡 Learning plan for a 5-year-old

    This is the Little Fox English series — 9 levels of increasing difficulty. For beginners, start with the gold zone: Level 1 → 2 → 3 (about 1.5–2 years of material). Save Level 4+ (Alice, Sherlock, novels) for later.

    🔤 Phonics path (across Levels 1→3)

    ABC Book (L1)→ Word Families (L1)→ Letter Teams (L2)→ Phonics I (L2)→ Phonics II (L3)

    Follow this order; by the time your child reaches Phonics II, they start to read on their own.

    📝 When to use subtitles? If your child cannot read yet → keep subtitles OFF (just listen + watch, which is why the default is off). Once they reach Phonics / recognize letters → turn captions ON in large size to "read along while listening", linking sounds to letters and speeding up reading. Captions also let parents read along to support the child.

    📚 4 learning stages

    1. Warm-up & language immersion: play background MP3 daily + watch the easiest short stories (Hello Cupcake, My First Readers 1) — keep subtitles OFF at this stage, just listen + watch, and rewatch many times.
    2. Letter & word families: ABC Book → Word Families → Letter Teams (play letter and word-family recognition games).
    3. Phonics — practice spelling: Phonics I → Phonics II (after plenty of listening and letter recognition).
    4. Reading & listening to stories: My First Readers 1→2→3, then Level 3 fairy tales (Cinderella, Snow White…).

    ⏱️ Daily rhythm (no pressure)

    • 🎧 Play background MP3 for 20–30 min whenever your child plays (language immersion).
    • 📺 Once a day watch 1–2 short captioned stories (~15 min).
    • 🔤 2–3 times a week one ABC / Word Families / Phonics episode.
    • ❤️ Rule #1: listen + imitate speaking first, phonics for reading — and stop when your child gets bored, keep it fun.
